Relative Architecture 10-Line Manifesto

  1. Organizations deliver outcomes by fulfilling responsibilities.

  2. Responsibilities originate in the business and product domain, not in technology.

  3. Architecture should model those responsibilities before modeling systems.

  4. Responsibilities may be fulfilled by people, partners, manual processes, or software capabilities.

  5. Capabilities are the software realization of a responsibility.

  6. Responsibilities collaborate by requesting outcomes and sharing facts.

  7. Requests express intent; events communicate what has happened.

  8. Domains organize responsibilities so teams can understand and govern them.

  9. System behavior emerges from networks of collaborating responsibilities.

  10. Technology should implement the responsibility model, not redefine it.
